WWDC 2026 Session: "What's New in Health and Wellbeing"
Session ID: 10142
Platform: iOS 20, watchOS 12

Transcript excerpt (condensed):

"This year we're introducing Journal Insights, a major expansion of the
Health app's journaling capabilities. Journal Insights uses on-device
machine learning to analyze your daily journal entries — the ones you write
in the Journal app or dictate via Siri — and automatically surfaces mood
trends over time.

Every entry is scored against a new Mood Graph, which correlates your
written reflections with signals like sleep quality, workout activity, and
calendar density. At the end of each week, Health surfaces a short written
summary: what lifted your mood, what patterns preceded low-mood days, and a
gentle, AI-generated prompt to help you reflect further tomorrow.

We've also added guided journaling prompts. Instead of a blank page, the
Journal app can now suggest a prompt tailored to your day — drawing on
calendar events, location visits, and even photos you took. And all of this
mood-trend data rolls up into a new Mood tab right inside the Health app,
alongside your existing sleep, activity, and mindfulness data — fully
exportable and shareable with a care provider.

This is built entirely on-device using the same Apple Neural Engine
pipeline that powers Personal Voice, so none of your journal content ever
leaves your iPhone unless you choose to share it."

Availability: iOS 20, watchOS 12, journaling and Mood tab enabled by
default for all Health app users. No opt-in required.
